## 1. The Service

Owloud is an English-learning app. You can listen to audiobooks and short
stories with synchronized ("karaoke-style") text, tap any word to see its
definition or Spanish translation, save words to a personal vocabulary, and
practice with spaced-repetition flashcards.

Chapters and short stories also come with a **quiz** built from what you
listened to — word matching, multiple-choice comprehension, listening
exercises, typed translation, word-bank and sentence-reorder exercises — and the
App records your best score for each one. You can also organize words into your
own collections, track your progress, statistics, and daily streak, and get
optional reminders.

Part of the App is free (see Section 6). Everything else is available with the
**Owloud Pro** subscription (see Section 5).
